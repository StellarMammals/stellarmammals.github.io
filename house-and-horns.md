		<p>After years of instrumental focus music, I started bringing live horns and vocals into deep, sun-soaked house, a sound I call <strong>house &amp; horns</strong>. Made in Venice Beach with Jacob Burgdorf on trumpet and LosA on vocals: warm, jazzy, unhurried house for warm-up sets and easy afternoons.</p>
